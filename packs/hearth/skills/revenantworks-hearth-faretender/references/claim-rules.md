## EU261 (EU Regulation 261/2004)

- Covers flights leaving an EU airport, and flights arriving at an EU airport on an EU carrier.
- Compensation is due when the flight arrives at the final destination 3 hours or more late, the cause is within the airline's control, and no extraordinary circumstances apply.
- Amounts per passenger by distance: EUR 250 up to 1,500 km; EUR 400 for 1,500 to 3,500 km; EUR 600 over 3,500 km.
- On flights over 3,500 km that arrive between 3 and 4 hours late, the airline may halve the amount (EUR 300). Status: Inferred from Article 7(2) and the UK CAA's matching rule; `verify` per case.
- Claims go to the airline, by a named passenger on the booking.

## UK261

- The UK kept the rule after leaving the EU, with amounts in GBP: GBP 220 for flights of 1,500 km or less; GBP 350 for 1,500 to 3,500 km; GBP 520 over 3,500 km (Article 7(1) as it applies in the UK).
- Over 3,500 km, a 3 to 4 hour late arrival pays GBP 260; more than 4 hours pays GBP 520 (UK CAA).
- Compensation needs an arrival more than 3 hours late. A delay of more than 5 hours also allows a refund instead of travel. No payment is due for extraordinary circumstances.
- The claim goes to the airline first. If it takes more than 8 weeks to respond, the passenger can escalate.
- Scope (which departures and carriers): `verify` against the UK261 annex before a draft states it.

## US DOT automatic refunds

- In force since 2024-10-28 (final rule issued April 2024).
- A cash refund is due when the airline cancels a flight or makes a significant change and the passenger does not travel or accept the change.
- Significant change: a domestic flight departs 3+ hours early or arrives 3+ hours late; an international flight 6+ hours; the origin or destination airport changes; connections increase; or the passenger is downgraded.
- Refund timing: 7 business days for card payments, 20 calendar days for other methods.
- Paid extras not received (seat choice, Wi-Fi) are refunded. Bag fees are refunded when the bag arrives more than 12 hours late on a domestic flight, or 15 or 30 hours on an international one, by flight length.
- Airlines may not replace a cash refund with a voucher or credit unless the passenger chooses it. A voucher offered instead must be valid for at least 5 years. Accepting a voucher or a rebooking can give up the refund right. Warn the user before they accept anything.
- The US has no fixed cash compensation for delays like EU261. Some airlines offer meal or hotel vouchers under their own commitments: `verify` per airline.

## Time limits

- EU261 sets no deadline; national law sets it, and it varies by country (the UK allows 6 years, 5 in Scotland, per claim services). Status: `verify` for the country where the claim would be heard.
