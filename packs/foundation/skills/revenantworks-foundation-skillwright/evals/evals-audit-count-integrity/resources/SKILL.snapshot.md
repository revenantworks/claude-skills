name: example-notes
description: Turns a pasted meeting transcript into numbered action items with an owner and a due date. Trigger on "action items from this meeting" or "who owes what". Not for writing the meeting agenda.
---

# example-notes

1. Read the transcript. 2. List each action as `N. <action> — <owner> — <due date or "no date">`.
If no owner is named, write `owner: unassigned`. Never invent a date.
