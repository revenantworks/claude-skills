## [1.0.0] — 2026-08-29

First release. First member of the `gamedev` pack (profile `standard`, motif `-smith`).

- **Band model** — near / mid / far by default, extended for a solar or star-map band, never renamed; the engineering half stays with the game.
- **Five laws** body-resident: drawn once read thrice · value before hue · silhouette carries near · grouping carries mid · the map owns far.
- **Four entries** — Direct (rule set), Test (`pixelsmith test`, one scene, two terrains, image or text path, scorecard), Brief (`pixelsmith brief`, artist brief and one-asset generator prompt), Audit (`pixelsmith audit`, score without redrawing — the drift-audit verb).
- **References** — `band-rules.md` (value ladder, per-band rules, N/M/F checklists), `look-test.md` (procedure, scorecard, pass line, fix order), `contrast.md` (the value-gap method with the 25-step pass, the fix ladder, and the case log opening with Case 1 — a hut at value 33 lost against forest at 37, fixed by a value shift to 80), `briefing.md` (templates and what a brief withholds).
- **Differentiators** — no incumbent skill scored art across zoom bands; sprite generators and single-scale guides stop at one scale. The text-described path makes the look test a full run on surfaces with no image tool.
- **Neutral** — no palette of any brand, no voice, no names; a brand palette arrives through brandwright and is never required.
- **Evals** — 20 trigger queries (10/10, four near-misses) and 12 assertion cases, authored, not run.
