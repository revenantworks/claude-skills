In our Godot colony sim we added an emergency ration: whenever the food store cannot cover a meal, the colonists eat seed grain instead. Since it shipped, a colony with a full granary went extinct on day 233. The designer says the rule is fine and only needs a smaller ration. Review the rule before we tune it.
